Version 1.0, in force from 15 October 2026.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data in the Award Monitoring & Tracking System is Yuriy Fedkovych Chernivtsi National University, 2 Kotsiubynskoho St., Chernivtsi, 58012, Ukraine. Questions about this notice and requests concerning your data go to the GDPR officer of the university: gdpr@chnu.edu.ua.

2. What data we process

  • Account data: university email address, first and last name, department, roles and their validity.
  • Awards and achievements you or your reviewers record, with their history, review decisions and the documents you upload.
  • Security data: sign-in devices, the activity log of your account and the audit trail of changes.
  • Notification preferences and the notifications sent to you.
  • Your consent records: which version of this notice you read, and your optional consents, with the time, IP address and browser of each change.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Recording, reviewing and publishing academic awards: the legitimate interest of the university in recognising the achievements of its staff and in institutional transparency (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Account security and the audit trail: legitimate interest and the legal obligations of the university to keep records of changes (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)).
  • Naming you in rankings and comparisons of unit reports (analytics): only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). Without it you are counted only in totals. You can give or withdraw this consent at any time in your profile; withdrawing does not affect earlier processing.

Confirming that you have read this notice is not a consent: it records that you were informed. Processing for the first two purposes does not depend on it.

4. Who receives your data

  • University staff with a role in the system, within their unit: department and faculty reviewers, administrators and the GDPR officer.
  • Anyone, including visitors without an account, for awards you mark as public.
  • Processors working for the university under contract: the hosting provider and the email delivery service. Your data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

  • Approved award records: permanently, as part of the university record; after the erasure of an account they are kept under a pseudonym.
  • Your profile: up to 7 years after your employment ends.
  • Uploaded documents: for the period you choose, from 1 to 10 years.
  • Consent records: 7 years after a consent is withdrawn.
  • System logs: 3 years. Audit trail: 7 years.

6. Your rights

  • Access your data and receive a copy in a machine-readable format (export in your profile).
  • Correct inaccurate data, and have your data erased where no legal reason to keep it applies.
  • Restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or with the data protection authority of the EU state where you live or work.

Write to gdpr@chnu.edu.ua to exercise any right; we answer within one month.

7. Changes to this notice

When this notice changes, it receives a new version number, and the system asks you to read the new version at your next sign-in. Earlier versions stay available on this page.

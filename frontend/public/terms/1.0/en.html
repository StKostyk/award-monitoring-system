Version 1.0, in force from 26 November 2026.

1. About the system

The Award Monitoring & Tracking System (the "system") belongs to Yuriy Fedkovych Chernivtsi National University, 2 Kotsiubynskoho St, Chernivtsi, 58012, Ukraine (the "university"). It is used to record, review and publish the academic awards and achievements of the university's staff and units. By using the system you accept these terms.

2. Who may use it

  • University staff with a university email address. Membership of a unit is confirmed by the faculty secretary or the head of the unit.
  • Roles (reviewer, secretary, dean and others) are granted by authorised persons according to the position held; delegated authority applies only within the limits and dates of the delegation.
  • Public awards can be viewed by anyone, without an account.

3. Your account

  • Your account is personal. Do not share your password and do not sign in with another person's account.
  • If you suspect that someone else used your account, tell the system administrator at once.
  • After several failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for a while; this protects you from password guessing.

4. What you enter

  • Enter only true information about awards, and documents that really support them and that you are allowed to upload.
  • Do not add other people's personal data beyond what the award record needs.
  • Uploaded files are checked for viruses; files that do not match their stated format or contain malicious code are refused.
  • An award you mark as public becomes visible to everyone; you can change its visibility at any time.

5. What is not allowed

  • Trying to view or change data your role does not give you access to, or to get around the system's protection.
  • Entering false achievements, or other people's achievements as your own.
  • Downloading data from the system automatically in amounts that disrupt its work.

6. Review and decisions

Decisions to approve, return or reject awards are taken by reviewers under the procedure in force at the university. The system records these decisions and their history but does not replace the university's official orders and documents.

7. Availability

The university aims to keep the system running without interruption but does not guarantee it: there may be breaks for updates and maintenance. Keep the originals of your documents outside the system. The university is not liable for indirect losses caused by interruptions, to the extent permitted by the law of Ukraine.

8. Breach of these terms

If these terms are breached, access to the account may be restricted under the university's internal rules. Actions in the system are recorded in the audit log.

9. Personal data

How the university processes your personal data is described in the privacy notice.

10. Changes to these terms

When the terms change, they get a new version number and a date from which they apply. The current version is always available on this page.

11. Governing law and contacts

These terms are governed by the law of Ukraine. Send questions about the system to the system administrator, and questions about personal data to gdpr@chnu.edu.ua.
